About Sudoku

This popular Japanese puzzle game is based entirely on logic. The goal is to fill a 9x9 grid with numbers so that every row, column, and 3x3 box contains all digits from 1 to 9. Playing daily improves your concentration and brain power. Start a game now to make it your favorite online puzzle.
