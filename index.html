Play Free Sudoku Now!

Sudoku is one of the most popular puzzle games of all time. The goal is to fill a 9x9 grid with numbers so that every row, column, and 3x3 box contains all digits from 1 to 9. Playing daily improves your concentration and brain power. Start a game now to make it your favorite online puzzle.

About Sudoku

This popular Japanese puzzle game is based entirely on logic. It does not require any calculation or special math skills. All you need is focus and a bit of brainpower.

How to play Sudoku

At the start of the game, the 9x9 grid will have some squares already filled. Your job is to logically fill in the missing numbers to complete the grid. Remember, a move is incorrect if:

  • Any row contains more than one of the same number from 1 to 9
  • Any column contains more than one of the same number from 1 to 9
  • Any 3x3 grid contains more than one of the same number from 1 to 9

Sudoku Tips

Learning to play can be slightly intimidating for beginners. If you are new, here are a few simple tips to improve your skills.

  • Tip 1: Look for rows, columns, or 3x3 sections that already have 5 or more numbers. Try placing the missing numbers in the empty cells. Often, you will find numbers that can only fit in one exact position.
  • Tip 2: Visually break the board into 3 large columns and 3 large rows. If two 3x3 boxes in a row already have the same number, the third box in that row must also contain it. Check the empty spots in that third box to find its exact location.
